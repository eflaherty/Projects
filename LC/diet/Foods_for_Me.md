## 🟡 Foods to Use More Carefully

These foods are on the approved food list but may be harder to incorporate into a low-FODMAP plan or may require portion control.

### Vegetables
- Artichokes
- Asparagus
- Brussels sprouts
- Cauliflower
- Larger servings of sweet potato
- Larger servings of butternut squash

### Beans and Legumes
- Kidney beans
- Black beans
- Navy beans
- Chickpeas
- Lentils

- Soak dried beans/legumes overnight in water.
- Pressure cook when possible.
- Use moderate portions.

### Nuts and Seeds

- Pine nuts
- Pistachios
- Sesame seeds

Respect the oxalate-related portion cautions in the food list.

# Practical Food-Handling Notes

For the low-histamine component, freshness and storage are important.

Instead of cooking meat once and keeping it refrigerated for several days:

1. Buy fresh meat.
2. Cook it promptly.
3. Eat what you need that day.
4. Freeze remaining portions promptly in individual servings.
5. Thaw only the portion needed for a meal.

Fresh, unaged meat and appropriate freezing/handling are especially important for the low-histamine approach.

# Using the Plan

Use the 🟢 foundation foods as the starting point rather than trying to eliminate every possible trigger.

A practical progression is:

**Phase 1:** Focus primarily on foundation foods.

**Phase 2:** Introduce 🟡 foods individually.

**Phase 3:** Identify personal tolerances and expand the diet as tolerated.

The goal is to find the least restrictive combination that works well for you rather than remaining on a highly restrictive diet indefinitely.
