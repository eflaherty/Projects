# Daily Rhythm
- 10 hour eating window (8 AM - 6 PM), 14 hours fasting

## Breakfast
- 8 - 10 AM (substantial cooked grains, easy to digest and warm with a little protein) - grounding & energizing

## Lunch
- 11:30 AM - 12:30 PM (full meal) - short 15 - 30 minutes rest (or more for bad sleep nights) after eating

## Dinner
- 5 PM - 6 PM - (lighter meal) lower on grains

## Bedtime
- Aim for 9 PM (going to bed consistently after 11 PM will deplete energy reserves) earlier in winter, later in summer
