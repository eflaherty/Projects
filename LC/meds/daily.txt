Daily Medication & Supplement Timing

Category key: 🔴 MCAS/histamine · 🟢 Gut health · 🔵 Hashimoto's · 🟣 Hormone (topical)

Morning — Empty Stomach, Right on Waking
- Armour Thyroid 🔵 — alone, with water only. Wait 30–60 minutes before eating, drinking anything else, or taking other supplements/meds.

Mid-Morning (after thyroid 1 - 2 hours, with or after food)
- Slippery elm 🟢 — between the thyroid window and the DAO/meal dose below; not stacked directly on minerals

Late-Morning (2 hours after slippery elm 2, with or after food)
- NAC 🟢 — relatively empty stomach, no specific conflicts
- Seeking Health S. boulardii 🟢 — take with food (any meal works); label is clean (ascorbyl palmitate, vegetarian capsule, silica) with no histamine-flagged fillers
- P5P (B6) 🔵 — flexible
- Liquid B12 🔵 — flexible

With Meals
- K2+D3 🔵 — with a meal containing some fat, for absorption
- Quercetin 🔴 — with a meal, ideally one with a bit of fat; often paired with DAO-style protocols
- Magnesium glycinate/malate 🔵 — keep at least 4 hours from Armour Thyroid; midday or with a meal works
- Zinc picolinate 🔵 — same 4-hour rule from thyroid; can go with magnesium

Afternoon
- LDN (3–5pm) 🔵 — as you're already doing; no known absorption conflicts with anything else on the list

Evening
- Zyrtec 🔴 — any time; evening if you notice drowsiness, otherwise flexible

Before Bed
- L-theanine 🔵 — flexible; some prefer it before a stressful period or later in the day for calming effect (stress reduction can indirectly help mast cell reactivity)
- Karuna Pure Progesterone 🟣 — topical, applied to skin (rotate sites: inner thighs/arms, neck, chest, abdomen)

Anytime (Topical, Skin-Applied)
- Quicksilver Bi-Est+ 🟣 — topical serum, 2 pumps once daily

As needed
- DAO product (Life Extension) 🔴 — right before meals, so it's active when dietary histamine is being digested
- Selenium (Thorne) 🔵 — flexible; no thyroid conflict (selenium is actually a thyroid cofactor), some take it alongside Armour Thyroid for that reason

The Two Firm Rules
Armour Thyroid — isolated, empty stomach, nothing else for 30–60 min minimum
Magnesium & zinc — kept at least 4 hours from the thyroid dose
